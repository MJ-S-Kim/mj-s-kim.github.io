About Me

Minjeong Kim

I’m a data scientist specializing in evaluation, calibration, and Python tooling for medical AI. I design multi-anchor threshold shifting and distributional-shift analyses, and maintain production-minded Python libraries (FROC/ROC/AFROC metrics, bootstrap CIs, DeLong) that make results reproducible and reviewable. I work closely with clinicians, engineers, and product teams to turn experiments into clear decision-support insights. I’m excited by roles where I can own an end-to-end slice—from problem framing and data design to reproducible code, docs, and measurable impact.
