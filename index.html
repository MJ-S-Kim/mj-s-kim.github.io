About

I specialize in evaluation and calibration for medical imaging AI, with a Ph.D. in Statistics (2025). My recent work focuses on threshold shifting (multi-anchor), distributional shift analysis, and practical evaluation metrics (FROC/ROC, bootstrap CIs, DeLong). I also study biomarker-based risk (e.g., ferritin and metabolic health) and the impact of data pseudonymization on analytic quality.
